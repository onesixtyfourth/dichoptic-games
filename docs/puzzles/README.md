Puzzle games are slower and let you take your time. The pieces of each puzzle are shared between your eyes, so you have to put the two pictures together to see the whole board. Most are timed or count your moves, and your best result is saved.

## Games

| Game                                          | What you do                                           | Weaker eye sees                | Stronger eye sees              |
|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| [Maze](maze.md)                               | Find a path from the start to the exit                | You and the exit               | Walls                          |
| [Memory Match](memory-match.md)               | Turn over cards to find matching pairs                | Card letters                   | Face-down cards                |
| [Sliding Puzzle](sliding-puzzle.md)           | Slide numbered tiles back into order                  | Odd-numbered tiles             | Even-numbered tiles            |
| [2048](2048.md)                               | Merge matching numbers to build bigger tiles          | Numbers                        | Tiles                          |
| [Sudoku](sudoku.md)                           | Fill the grid with 1 to 9                             | Numbers you enter              | Given numbers                  |
| [Mine Sweeper](mine-sweeper.md)               | Uncover every square that has no mine                 | Number clues and flags         | Covered squares                |
| [Bubble Shooter](bubble-shooter.md)           | Fire bubbles to make groups of three                  | The bubble you fire            | Bubbles on the board           |
| [Link Pairs](link-pairs.md)                   | Join matching tiles with a short path                 | Half of the symbols            | The other half                 |
| [Spot the Difference](spot-the-difference.md) | Find shapes that look different to each eye           | Its own version of each difference | Its own version of each difference |
| [Shape Sort](shape-sort.md)                   | Sort shapes so each tube holds one kind               | Half of the kinds of shape     | The other half                 |
| [Simon](simon.md)                             | Repeat a growing sequence of lit panels               | Half of the panels             | The other half                 |
| [Mahjong Solitaire](mahjong-solitaire.md)     | Remove free matching tiles from the stacks            | Half of the tile symbols       | The other half                 |
| [Shape Crush](shape-crush.md)                 | Swap shapes to make lines of three                    | Shapes on alternate squares    | Shapes on the other squares    |
| [Picture Logic](picture-logic.md)             | Use number clues to reveal a hidden picture           | Number clues                   | The grid you fill in           |

## Good to know

* The difficulty setting usually changes the size of the board or the number of pieces.
* For games where a lower result is better (fewer moves or a faster time), your best is the lowest you have managed.
* Puzzles that use a cursor (a white square) can be played with a keyboard or controller as well as by touch.
