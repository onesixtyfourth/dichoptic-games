Strategy games are about planning rather than quick reactions. Most are turn-based, so there's no rush. In most of them your pieces are shown to your weaker eye and the computer's pieces to your stronger eye. To plan a move you have to look at both sets together.

## Games

| Game                                | What you do                                     | Weaker eye sees          | Stronger eye sees                 |
|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|--------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| [Tic Tac Toe](tic-tac-toe.md)       | Get three in a row on a 3 × 3 board             | Your X marks             | The computer's O marks            |
| [Four in a Row](four-in-a-row.md)   | Drop discs to line up four                      | Your discs               | The computer's discs              |
| [Gomoku](gomoku.md)                 | Get five in a row on an 11 × 11 board           | Your stones              | The computer's stones             |
| [Solitaire](solitaire.md)           | Build each suit from Ace to King                | Red cards                | Black cards and face-down cards   |
| [Reversi](reversi.md)               | Trap the computer's pieces to flip them         | Your pieces              | The computer's pieces             |
| [Checkers](checkers.md)             | Jump and capture all the computer's pieces      | Your pieces              | The computer's pieces             |
| [Battleships](battleships.md)       | Find and sink the computer's fleet              | Hits                     | Misses and your ships             |
| [Dots and Boxes](dots-and-boxes.md) | Draw lines to complete and claim boxes          | Your lines and boxes     | The computer's lines and boxes    |
| [Mancala](mancala.md)               | Sow stones and capture more than the computer   | Your stones              | The computer's stones             |
| [Chess](chess.md)                   | Checkmate the computer's king                   | Your pieces              | The computer's pieces             |
| [Tower Defence](tower-defence.md)   | Build towers to stop waves of invaders          | Invaders                 | Road, towers and shots            |

## Good to know

* Except for Solitaire and Tower Defence, every game is a series of rounds against the computer. After each round there is a short pause and a new round starts. The top bar keeps count of your wins, losses and draws.
* Who goes first swaps each round.
* The difficulty setting changes how well the computer plays. On Easy it often makes random moves; on Hard it looks further ahead.
* Your best score is the most rounds you have won in one sitting. In Tower Defence it is the most waves you have cleared.
