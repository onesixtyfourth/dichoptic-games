Arcade games are fast and keep both eyes busy. The moving things you have to react to are usually shown to your weaker eye, while the scenery or your own character is shown to your stronger eye. Most of them speed up the longer you play.

## Games

| Game                                  | What you do                                         | Weaker eye sees              | Stronger eye sees         |
|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| [Blocks](blocks.md)                   | Fit falling pieces together to clear lines          | Falling piece and next piece | Stack of landed blocks    |
| [Snake](snake.md)                     | Steer a growing snake to the food                   | Snake                        | Food                      |
| [Brick Breaker](brick-breaker.md)     | Bounce a ball off your paddle to break bricks       | Ball and paddle              | Bricks                    |
| [Table Tennis](table-tennis.md)       | Beat the computer to 7 points                       | Ball                         | Both paddles              |
| [Flap](flap.md)                       | Fly a bird through gaps in the walls                | Bird                         | Walls                     |
| [Runner](runner.md)                   | Jump and duck past obstacles                        | Obstacles                    | Runner                    |

| [Space Shooter](space-shooter.md)     | Steer an auto-firing ship and dodge enemy fire      | Enemies and their shots      | Your ship and shots       |

## Good to know

* The difficulty setting changes speed, size or the number of hazards. Each game page lists what changes.
* Most arcade games can be played with the on-screen buttons, by touching the game area, with a keyboard or with a game controller.
* Your best score for each game is saved on your phone and shown in the game menu.
